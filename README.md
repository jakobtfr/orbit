# NASA Space Apps Challenge 2026 — Health Monitoring for Astronauts

> Working repo for the 2026 NASA Space Apps Challenge.
> Challenge: **Create Health Monitoring Software for Astronauts on Space Missions**

## The challenge in one line

Build a visual tool that lets astronauts **collect their own health indicators, understand what they mean, and act on them** during long-duration missions — without leaning on a flight surgeon on the ground.

## Why it matters

Long-duration missions expose crews to space radiation, isolation and confinement, altered gravity, and a hostile closed environment. The consequences include immune changes, bone loss, cardiovascular events, and behavioral health problems. As missions get longer and communication delays grow, astronauts carry more of the responsibility for spotting changes in themselves.

| **Validity** | Do data, methods, and assumptions support the result? | Published HRP thresholds, cited evidence per indicator, deterministic and explainable rules |
| **Presentation** | Can judges understand it and see it in action? | Scripted mission simulation with injected anomalies, live demo |
