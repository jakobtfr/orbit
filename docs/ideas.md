# Concept Notes

Working scratchpad — update as decisions are made.

## What the challenge actually rewards

1. **Self-service assessment**, not a dashboard for doctors. The astronaut is the user.
2. **Guidance after the number** — "what do I do now?"
3. **Visual clarity** — status at a glance, drill down for explanation.
4. **Evidence** — indicator choices and thresholds must trace back to NASA HRP / space medicine literature.
5. **A demo judges can feel** — a simulated mission with a health event beats a static UI.

## Differentiators to consider

- **Autonomy under comms delay.** Package a "crew health handover" for the next ground contact; the astronaut gets guidance offline now.
- **Explainable triage.** Deterministic rules with "why this matters in microgravity" evidence per indicator, not a black-box score.
- **Low-friction capture.** Voice / large touch targets / camera, designed for a cramped, low-light habitat.
- **Mission-routine coaching.** Nudges and light gamification framed as preserving mission readiness, not fitness app streaks.
- **Crew-level view.** One crew of 4, shared trends, so a crewmate can notice what an individual misses.
